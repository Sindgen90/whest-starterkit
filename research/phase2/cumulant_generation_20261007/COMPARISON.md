## Ranked omissions and next mathematical target

1. **Independent raw-moment stabilization was defective and is now isolated/fixed.** Exact positive-ReLU recentering requires deltaM4=4*m*kappa3+kappa4. Calculate centered corrections and recenter consistently; do not bound raw M3/M4 independently. The coherent layer attenuation scales all incoming third/fourth quantities and their nonlinear interactions consistently, but shows no convincing additional benefit here.
2. **Our third sources omit distinct-index content at birth and then discard old history.** For Gaussian input to a ReLU, the leading three-distinct third cumulant is the sum of three covariance-tree terms w2_i*w1_j*w1_k*Cij*Cik plus cyclic permutations. Our Sym(A,I,I) birth is zero there. The public Sym(3A,I,A*diag(w2)) birth includes it and keeps compressed older sources. Existing four-stream small-network references reduce distinct-index birth risk from **0.00038324544 to 2.4518452e-06** with that leading tree. Its next-weight marginal-contraction risk is within reference noise (a negative cross-stream estimate is not negative physical error). This is small-network mechanism evidence, not a tested new width1024 candidate.
3. **The public contracted fourth core and lower-cumulant interactions were absent from our earlier comparisons.** Its G is not paired Q. G feeds new third sources; its mean table includes third-squared and third-fourth products. Complete V25/V29 reproduction is therefore essential; Q/R/block tests do not test the same mechanism. The source map, conditional exact affine identity, empirical lambda rule, projection losses, and licensing are in MATHEMATICS.md. Hanin's cumulant identities transfer to fixed weights; his random-weight cancellations and width guarantees do not.
4. **For the reproduced public method, accumulated mean/variance error is the strongest remaining diagnostic lead.** On development's final layer, its marginal response formula gives MSE **2.1879038e-08**. Replacing just incoming mean/variance with reference values gives **2.9724328e-09**; replacing only incoming third gives **2.0570472e-08**, only effective fourth **2.0587891e-08**, and all four **9.632847e-10**. Fresh-reference mean noise is approximately0.7–1.0e-9 on these networks, so the all-oracle result is near that scale. These are one-step offline interventions, not deployable improvements or additive error attribution. Earlier fourth-core feedback can affect all subsequent means/covariances. Investigate covariance update consistency and the effect of compressed third births on that update before adding tensor formats or higher cumulant orders.

The public diagnostic formula matches logged means within 5.42e-07 absolute. C is captured after its diagonal is zeroed in place; v2 compares covariance **off diagonal**. The v1 diagnostic JSON/script remain preserved but their covariance_mse includes a known zero-diagonal artifact; their other metrics are unaffected. No reference target enters any estimator.
