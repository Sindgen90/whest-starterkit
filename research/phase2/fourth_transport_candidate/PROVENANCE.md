# Fourth transport research replica

3 October 2026. This is an offline research replica of the frozen
`../joint_full_runtime/estimator.py`, not a submission folder.
It imports `../fourth_transport.py` and compares the original Gram transport
against the complete transport of the paired-only fourth-tensor projection.
The Gram path matches the frozen diagnostic state and means exactly.
`collect_diagnostics=True` records covariance/fourth planes for the study.

All other moment formulas, quadrature, history and clipping are unchanged.
Monte Carlo reference moments are generated by the separate diagnostic script
after predictions, and never enter either estimator recurrence. No coefficients
are fitted. This experiment did not meet its predeclared improvement criterion,
and was not promoted or packaged. The successful estimator and archive remain
byte-identical. See `../FOURTH_TRANSPORT_DIAGNOSTIC.md`.

The inherited MIT license is retained. Upstream covariance baseline:
https://github.com/agentaiventure-dot/arc-whest-estimator,
commit `fb33bb5a66b4b0aed3022e6a70a9274d5957d61d`.
The joint closure is our local approximate extension inspired by the Puffi A716
write-up, not a reproduction of its submission.
